# Tower Defense audio sources

All audio shipped in this directory is stored locally and may be used in commercial builds.

## Music (CC0 1.0)

- `forest-gate-ambience.mp3` — `forest_2.ogg` from **Cathedral in the forest (ambient loop)** by congusbongus: https://opengameart.org/content/cathedral-in-the-forest-ambient-loop
- `northern-pass-ambience.mp3` — `lost.ogg` from **Lost in a bad place (horror ambience loop)** by congusbongus: https://opengameart.org/content/lost-in-a-bad-place-horror-ambience-loop
- `boss-intro.mp3` and `boss-battle.mp3` — intro and loop files from **Heartfelt Battle** by request: https://opengameart.org/content/heartfelt-battle-loopable-fantasy-stringspianohorn

## Sound effects (CC0 1.0)

The production effects are selected, mixed and converted from these packs:

- **Fantasy Sound Effects (Tinysized SFX)** by Ján Schupke / Vehicle: https://opengameart.org/content/fantasy-sound-effects-tinysized-sfx
- **RPG Audio** by Kenney: https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
- **Impact Sounds** by Kenney: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
- **Interface Sounds** by Kenney: https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds

License: Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The source WAV/OGG files were trimmed, layered where appropriate, converted to MP3 and renamed for the game. Attribution is not required by CC0, but source information is retained here for provenance.
