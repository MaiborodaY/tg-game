# Goblin Bombardier

The first biome's mini-boss is replaced on wave 10 of rounds 1-11 through 1-19.
Rounds 1-10 and 1-20 retain their Ogre main boss. Earlier mini-bosses and the
entire second biome keep their existing enemy types.

Goblin Cave II also uses this boss in its third and final wave. Its dungeon-only
HP/damage use the round 1-10 campaign boss baseline plus 30%; the three-wave run
awards 300 gold and 5 slaves per clear. The campaign encounters remain unchanged.

The replacement preserves each encounter's resolved HP, damage, reward, escort,
opening healer, arrival schedule and total enemy count. The Bombardier shoots
one target at 135 range with the previous mini-boss's base 2.15-second cooldown,
1.4-second action and 40 movement speed, all using the common combat pace.
Pose 2 releases a bomb at 55% of the action. One hit is applied on arrival through
the shared projectile/armour/shield rules. The blast is visual, with no extra
splash, poison or stun. A released bomb can outlive its caster; battle completion
cancels outstanding projectiles. Allied melee and hero pursuit treat it as ranged.

The supplied 512px body and two 128px effects are loaded by the shared scene cache
only for waves containing the boss. Authored crops, wheel anchors, shot rows,
mirrored muzzle position and the existing 59.8-unit body size are retained.
The four-frame explosion runs once for 0.4 seconds. Production does not load
source PNGs, GIFs, concepts, or the standalone preview page.
