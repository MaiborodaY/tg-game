Use case: stylized-concept, production modular equipment sprites for Forest Forge.
Input 1 is the actual hero BODY PARTS LAYOUT AND GEOMETRY. Input 2 is the APPROVED COSTUME CONCEPT SHEET; use ONLY the character specified below.
Generate ONE square 1536x1536 sheet with a strictly regular 4x4 grid of EXACTLY THIRTEEN COMPLETE ISOLATED ARMOR PIECES. Each cell is 384x384. Leave at least 28 px clear space between every silhouette and its cell border. The three deliberately empty cells MUST remain empty. Never assemble a hero or include any skin, hair or face. Each piece matches the facing direction, handedness and proportions of its corresponding body template.

Cell layout:
Row 1: [HEADGEAR ONLY, fitted CHEST ONLY neck to waist, short CAPE swept left, EMPTY].
Row 2: [REAR SHOULDER, FRONT SHOULDER, complete CLOSED REAR GLOVE, complete CLOSED FRONT GLOVE].
Row 3: [REAR HIP PANEL, FRONT HIP PANEL, REAR LOWER-LEG COVERING WITHOUT FOOT, FRONT LOWER-LEG COVERING WITHOUT FOOT].
Row 4: [complete REAR SHORT ANKLE BOOT, complete FRONT SHORT ANKLE BOOT, EMPTY, EMPTY].

Headgear is the hat/helmet only, in the approved 3/4 RIGHT-facing view. No head, face, hair, ears, neck or skin. The game supplies the head beneath it. Keep the face opening clear white/transparent and broad enough to show both eyes. Hat brim undersides are thin flat shapes, not big black recessed cavities. Helmet side plates follow the concept but never add chin guards, face masks or interior socket shading.
Chest ends at the waist with a complete finished bottom edge; no attached shoulders, arms, hands, long skirts or hip flaps. Hip panels are two COMPLETE separate cloth/armor shapes fitted to left/right upper legs, never a combined pair of trousers.
Lower-leg coverings fit the template shins and end above the boots, with finished exterior rounded edges. No soles or toes, hollow tubes, open holes or feet attached.
Gloves cover complete fists including palm and cuff, as seen from OUTSIDE in the template orientation. A simple closed mitten silhouette with one thumb lump. No skin stumps, hollow cuffs, open sockets, detached wristbands, complicated fingers or holes.
Boots are complete SHORT ankle footwear with closed toes, heels, soles and broad flat cuff bands. Leggings and boots MUST NOT be combined. No open oval tops, recessed dark rims, hollow interiors or cropped foot fragments. Rear and front boots follow their corresponding different bare-foot orientations.
Every sprite has its own continuous clean near-black outlined contour. Body-template straight crop edges are only guides, not final equipment boundaries.

Match the approved chunky juicy cartoon finish: saturated color, thick crisp near-black outlines, two or three broad cel-shaded regions and large clean highlights. No tiny rivets, seams, stitching, buttons, texture, speckles, camouflage patterns or engraving. Preserve the chosen set's exact shapes, palette and lighting. Assets must remain readable on the existing roughly 39px game hero.
Genuinely transparent alpha background. If alpha cannot be produced use pure #FFFFFF white, including ALL negative-space gaps. NEVER checkerboard, ivory/gray background, cast shadows, grid lines, labels, text, watermarks, body parts, weapons or shields.

Select ONLY the LEFT FIELD SCOUT. Headgear: olive-green soft field cap with short right-facing brim, broad khaki band, no hair. Chest: compact olive vest, two large flat tan chest panels, clean waist belt; no shoulder pads or sleeves attached. Cape: short moss-green cloth, one darker fold. Shoulders: rounded olive exterior caps. Gloves: full closed brown leather mittens, flat brown cuff band. Hip panels: two separate olive cloth flaps with simple khaki shading. Lower-leg coverings: olive knee/shin guards over tan cloth, ending above footwear. Boots: complete short brown leather ankle boots with broad brown cuff bands and black soles, no shin guards attached.
