Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: a THREE-WEAPON fantasy concept sheet for the small 2D browser game Forest Forge.

Reference image 1 (Underworld armor) and reference image 2 (Divine armor) are DRAWING-STYLE REFERENCES ONLY: their bold near-black outlines, appealing chunky cartoon proportions and readable broad color planes. Do not draw any of their heroes, clothing, weapon-like armor decorations, text, layouts or scenery.

PRIMARY REQUEST: exactly THREE radically imaginative handheld fantasy weapons. Each must have a different underlying CONSTRUCTION and instantly different BLACK SILHOUETTE. The previous weapon batch was rejected as too generic: familiar swords, axes, hammers and guns with alternate colors/materials. This time the fantasy idea must define the entire object, not be an ornament stuck onto a conventional weapon.

Layout: ONE landscape 1536 x 1024 sheet, THREE evenly separated vertical columns, one complete weapon per column, in the order below. Each isolated weapon stays within its own column with at least 45px blank margin; all tips and grips visible. Center them around the same horizontal axis, but keep genuinely different proportions. No drawn cell boundaries, labels, letters, numbers, character, hands, ground, shadows, particles or scenery.

LEFT — TITAN FIST (melee, existing heavy swing):
A petrified ancient GIANT'S CLENCHED FIST, shaped into a handheld striking relic. The entire weapon is the fist and a SHORT broken stone wrist; there is NO separate hammer head, club shaft or long pole. The massive knuckles form a blunt stepped diagonal ridge; an oversized thumb wraps across the front, leaving one large notch in the silhouette. Three-quarter SIDE view, fist raised upper-left, thick wrist angled down-right, with a short dark leather wrap around the narrow wrist as the practical handgrip. The upper fist is at least three times wider than the grip. Warm ochre sandstone, mossy olive recessed planes, only ONE broad amber fissure across two knuckles; large planar stone shapes, minimal carving. A huge compact asymmetrical block silhouette. It must unmistakably read as an impossible titan-fist relic, not a boxing glove, human hand illustration or ordinary mace.

MIDDLE — DEMON WING (melee, existing heavy swing):
A complete small demon's rigid unfolded BAT WING used as a cutting weapon. The broad crimson-black MEMBRANE ITSELF forms the striking surface; NO metal axe blade, sword blade, polearm or crescent blade. ONE large sweeping triangular wing, three thick ivory finger-bones fanning outward from a pronounced elbow, ending in ONLY three very large hooked wing tips. Two deep scallops along the outer membrane edge create strong negative space. A short crooked dark plum forearm bone below the wing forms a usable grip with a simple red leather wrap. The wing leans conspicuously to the RIGHT of the grip and is much WIDER than a blade, thin in profile, with one huge warm-red membrane plane and one dark wine-red shadow plane. A dramatic broad fan silhouette. Avoid feathers, many little spines, tiny veins or ornate engravings. This is a single wing-shaped handheld relic, not a living full creature.

RIGHT — BOMBARDIER BEETLE (ranged, existing horizontal shooting pose):
A bizarre LIVING HANDHELD BEETLE CANNON seen from the side, aiming RIGHT. The whole gun IS a stout fantasy beetle. Its huge high bulbous shell/abdomen occupies the LEFT two-thirds, like a rounded iridescent green seed pod with two very broad turquoise plates and a single amber seam. Its small head projects low to the RIGHT, with two short blunt mandibles framing a dark forward-facing natural nozzle. NO metal barrel, rifle stock, trigger guard, scope or normal gun body. Two thick folded belly legs join into one short brown U-shaped handle under the rear half; the hero could hold it using the existing gun grip. The remaining limbs are tucked tightly against the shell, not spread like a walking insect. One small amber eye makes it feel alive. Highly distinctive horizontal teardrop silhouette with a high rounded rear and low forward mouth, strongly different from both upright melee objects. No spray, projectile, detached eggs, wings or floating effects.

Art direction: extravagant fantasy but CLEAN, CHEERFUL and BOLD, readable at 40px. Thick confident outline, simple curved shapes, 2–3 broad cel-shaded planes per material. No photorealism, 3D-render sheen, polygon-facet noise, scratches, filigree, gore, tiny runes, magic aura, bloom or glow outside the object. Each object must look interesting without any effects. Color supports the design; it must not carry the distinction. Every grip stays clearly visible and belongs naturally to the object.

Background: genuine transparent alpha including enclosed empty holes if supported; otherwise perfectly flat pure white #FFFFFF including the holes. Never a painted checkerboard, paper texture, grey gradient or vignette. Draw exactly the three requested isolated weapons, no duplicates or comparison miniatures.
