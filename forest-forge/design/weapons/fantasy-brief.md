# Next fantasy weapon batch — direction and first preview

The user found the seven-epoch batch too uniform and wants substantially more fantasy. Keep the current integrated catalog; the next batch adds more distinctive designs.

- Each item starts from a different fantasy construction, not a conventional sword/axe/hammer/gun plus a new material.
- Silhouettes must remain distinguishable in solid black at 40px, without relying on color, gems, surface patterns or glow.
- Vary mass distribution, length, negative space, outline direction and asymmetry within the sheet.
- Do not repeat the previous six-template sequence across epochs. Compare against the existing 69 weapons.
- Keep a visible usable grip and a coherent silhouette. Existing swing, thrust, combined sword, bow and horizontal shooting poses only.
- Thick outlines and two or three broad planes; no small decoration or glow used to manufacture variety.
- The user reduced the first preview to three weapons: two melee and one ranged. Judge its silhouettes before generating several epochs.
