# Forge: 60 levels

Runtime source: balance.mjs; matching table: year-one-anvil.csv.

Existing anvil levels and running upgrade deadlines are preserved. Former level 40 can upgrade further; maximum is 60.

Medieval opens at level 4 at 0.02%; later epochs open every six levels when the previous reaches 20%. Divine opens at 46 and reaches 5% at 60. Final distribution: Interdimensional 20%, Underworld 75%, Divine 5%.

Timers grow to 24h at level 30, 36h at 40, 48h at 50 and 72h at 60. Total timers: 97,776 minutes (67.9 days), excluding saving coins and time between upgrades.

Coin prices follow the September 13 mob-income update (+50% between biomes, +1% per stage within later biomes). Levels 1–5 retain their previous prices. Relative to the previous price table, the multiplier rises linearly between these anchors: level 5 ×1, 10 ×1.5, 20 ×2, 30 ×3, 40 ×4, 50 ×5, 60 ×5. Each final price is rounded up to ten coins and stored directly in the runtime and CSV tables.

Total cost: 165,985,880 coins. At the final ordinary mob's 2,108 coins, the final upgrade costs about 11,860 kills, versus about 12,756 before this update (5,000,000 / 392). This compares ordinary mob income only; bosses, sales and other income are separate. Existing coin balances, already-paid upgrade deadlines, timers and item chances are unchanged. Coin-based timer skipping continues to use the existing formula based on the upgrade price and remaining time.
