# Forge: 60 levels

Runtime source: balance.mjs; matching table: year-one-anvil.csv.

Existing anvil levels and running upgrade deadlines are preserved. Former level 40 can upgrade further; maximum is 60.

Medieval opens at level 4 at 0.02%; later epochs open every six levels when the previous reaches 20%. Divine opens at 46 and reaches 5% at 60. Final distribution: Interdimensional 20%, Underworld 75%, Divine 5%.

Timers grow to 24h at level 30, 36h at 40, 48h at 50 and 72h at 60. Total timers: 97,776 minutes (67.9 days), excluding saving coins and time between upgrades.

Prices through level 30 retain the approved early schedule. Subsequent geometric segments reach 250,000 at 40, 1,000,000 at 50 and 5,000,000 at 60, rounded up to ten coins. Total cost: 33,965,920 coins.
